The biggest risk to a Hong Kong number is not the cost — it is silent recycling. Once the number is reassigned, every bank, ChatGPT and app account tied to that +852 verification number goes dark. This guide only covers products that actually come with a Hong Kong number: in our directory that is [Club Sim](/sims/club-sim/) and [3HK DIY](/sims/3hk-diy/), both official carrier prepaid platforms. esim.cc and Yoho Mobile are data-only eSIMs with no number, so they are outside this discussion — explained below.

One-line conclusion: **for pure number parking, Club Sim has the clearest rule and the lowest cost**. 3HK DIY's value is the official carrier identity and mainland roaming experience, but you have to watch its balance yourself.

## Club Sim: the official HK$15-a-year renewal

Club Sim's rule is official and simple: buy any pack within 365 days of your last purchase and the number stays yours. The cheapest renewal is the HK$15 Asia 24-hour roaming pass — one purchase a year, about HK$15 annually.

Three traps worth knowing:

- **New-SIM orders carry a HK$50 minimum charge**: the HK$28 pack requires topping up to HK$50, or take the HK$58 tier directly. First-year cost is higher than the "HK$15 a year" intuition suggests.
- **Real-name registration is mandatory**: unregistered SIMs get suspended under HK law; visitors can register with a passport in the app.
- **The 365-day window is hard**: miss it and the number is permanently deactivated. Set a calendar reminder at month 11, not month 12.

For mainland use, Club Sim sells a dedicated [China-Macau roaming zone](/sims/club-sim/): 1GB/HK$38 or 3GB/HK$88 with 90-day validity, and the operator advertises unrestricted social-app access. Note that VoLTE requires a voice pack, and the local voice pack explicitly excludes roaming calls.

## 3HK DIY: official carrier identity, but you watch the balance

[3HK DIY](/sims/3hk-diy/) is the official prepaid product of Hong Kong carrier 3, giving you a real 3HK local number. Its strength is mainland roaming: the official app configurator sells custom travel passes for Asia-Pacific (mainland included) from HK$13/day plus HK$8 per extra 500MB, valid up to 180 days (verified on the official configurator, 2026-09). Receiving SMS in the mainland is free, sending costs HK$3.5 (reseller wording); enable data roaming on insertion and the traffic exits via Hong Kong mobile networks.

On keep-alive, honesty first: **the rule is community-quoted, not independently verified against official terms** — balance validity is tied to pack purchases, and resellers cite roughly HK$200/year in top-ups. The practical routine is checking balance expiry in the app every 90–180 days and completing a top-up or pass purchase inside the window.

Two structural limits: plan selection, payment and activation all happen inside the app with no public price table, so you cannot compare before installing; and coverage is Asia-centric, not global.

## Why esim.cc and Yoho are not in this discussion

[esim.cc](/sims/esimcc/) and [Yoho Mobile](/sims/yoho-esim/) are data-only eSIMs — **no phone number, so there is nothing to keep alive**. They solve "using HK/international data from the mainland", not number parking. esim.cc's China day pass ($2.99/day, 2GB high-speed then 128kbps unlimited) and China annual plan ($49.90, 100GB/365 days) are genuinely cheap, landing on China Mobile 4G/5G with no real-name requirement; community single-user reports put its landing IP in Hong Kong (unverified), and the eSIM locks to the device. Fine as a data tool, useless for parking a number.

## FAQ

**Minimum total cost for verification SMS only?** Club Sim route: ~HK$50 first order (minimum charge) plus HK$15/year renewals — about HK$65 in year one, HK$15/year after. 3HK DIY lives entirely in the app; the community quote is ~HK$200/year.

**Receiving SMS from the mainland?** Both work. On Club Sim, check your voice pack and VoLTE status; on 3HK DIY, enable data roaming and receiving codes is free — but finish in-app real-name registration first.

**What if I miss the window?** Club Sim deactivates permanently and the number may be reassigned — you would need a new SIM and to re-bind every account. That is why this guide keeps pushing calendar reminders.

**Who should pick 3HK DIY?** Frequent mainland travellers who want China roaming data packs or an official carrier network. Pure parking users are better served by Club Sim.
