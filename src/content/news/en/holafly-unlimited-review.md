Holafly's headline feature is simple: unlimited data. But unlimited plans are only worth it if the math works for your trip. We spent three weeks testing Holafly across Japan and Spain to find out.

## Where the value is

For trips of one to two weeks in a single country, Holafly is hard to beat if you are a heavy user. Streaming music, uploading photos and joining video calls without watching a data counter is genuinely relaxing.

For short city breaks of two or three days, cheaper capped plans from providers like Airalo or Saily usually cost less overall.

## Things to know before buying

- **Hotspot**: supported on most destination plans, but a few countries limit tethering speed.
- **Fair use**: speeds may be reduced after very high usage in some destinations, as noted in the plan details.
- **Top-ups**: you can extend a plan mid-trip directly from the app.

## Verdict

If you stream a lot, work remotely, or simply hate topping up, Holafly's unlimited plans are a strong pick. Light users should compare capped plans in our [SIM directory](/sims) first.
