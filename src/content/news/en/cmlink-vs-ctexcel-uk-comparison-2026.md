For Chinese speakers living in the UK, [CMLink UK](/sims/cmlink-uk) (China Mobile) and [CTExcel UK](/sims/ctexcel-uk) (China Telecom) are the two default candidates. Both are EE MVNOs, both offer a UK number plus a Chinese secondary number, and both target the UK-China corridor. The differences that matter: keep-alive cost, price per GB back to China, 5G, and IP landing. All official facts below verified 2026-09-26.

## The short answer

- **Want 5G and reliable SMS verification while in mainland China** → CMLink
- **Buy large China data bundles and want the cheapest keep-alive** → CTExcel
- **Minimum upfront cost** → CTE UK (the budget CTExcel-family monthly line, see below)

## Core comparison

- **Host network**: both ride EE, identical UK coverage
- **5G**: CMLink supports it; CTExcel roams on 4G only (official)
- **Dual number**: both offer UK + Chinese secondary number for SMS in the mainland
- **First activation**: both require UK/EU presence; CTExcel additionally needs chargeable activity within 10 days of activation
- **China data pricing (official, 2026-09)**:
  - CMLink China bundle: 40GB shared UK+China / £19 / 90 days
  - CTExcel China bundles: 30GB/£22 and 50GB/£28 (promo, was £36/£40) / 180 days — clearly cheaper per GB

## Keep-alive: the biggest difference

**CMLink**: monthly/annual plans get 90 days after expiry before the line is closed; but tourist/one-off China bundles get only **30 days** (confirmed in the official planlist wording, 2026-09). Easy to miss.

**CTExcel**: any chargeable event in a **180-day** window keeps the line alive — a call, text, data, top-up or bundle purchase all count, and texting BAL to 888 (balance check) counts too. Official reminder SMS at ~60 days of inactivity. One of the lowest keep-alive bars among UK Chinese-community SIMs.

For a parked number, CTExcel wins outright.

## IP landing and verification use (community-reported)

- Both exit as native UK EE mobile IPs when in the UK
- CMLink mainland-China roaming reportedly lands on a **Hong Kong residential IP**, with Singapore breakout on Singapore plans (single-user hands-on, 2026-09, unverified) — the reason it doubles as an AI-subscription backup card
- CTExcel mainland landing is "similar to CMLink" per community, but not independently verified; the operator advertises a "clean UK IP"

## Which plans for whom (official, 2026-09)

CMLink for: UK-based users who want 5G speeds, visit China occasionally, and value stable verification SMS. Entry point is a £1 trial with a £2 standard activation top-up.

CTExcel for: heavy China-data users (30-50GB per half-year) on a 180-day cycle, plus Wi-Fi Calling with unlimited calls to China. Note the 365-day bundles (£15/15GB, £36/50GB) are discontinued; only 180-day bundles remain.

## What is CTE UK?

[CTE UK](/sims/cteuk) is China Telecom Europe's other product line. Official pricing: 22GB UK-China shared at £9.90/30 days up to 300GB at £19.90; freecard promo gives a free SIM with £1 prepay waived — the lowest entry cost of the three. The tradeoffs: 90-day activity rule and community stability reports behind CMLink. Open with CTE, park long-term with CMLink or CTExcel.

## FAQ

**Can I activate in mainland China?** No for both — plan for activation on arrival in the UK/EU.

**Which receives verification codes more reliably in China?** Community consensus favors CMLink (EE roaming, Hong Kong residential landing); CTExcel works but its mainland landing is unverified.

**Does 5G matter?** Only if you live in a UK city and use data heavily. For parking and SMS, 4G is fine.
