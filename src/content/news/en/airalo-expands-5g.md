Airalo, one of the largest eSIM marketplaces, announced this week that its 5G coverage has expanded to 30 more destinations, including popular travel spots across Southeast Asia, Eastern Europe and South America.

## What this means for travelers

With this expansion, Airalo's total 5G footprint now covers more than 80 destinations. Travelers holding compatible devices can expect noticeably faster speeds when streaming, video-calling or navigating in the newly added countries.

No configuration is needed: eSIMs purchased for supported destinations automatically connect to 5G networks where available, falling back to 4G/LTE elsewhere.

## Pricing unchanged

The expansion comes at no extra cost. Regional plans covering multiple countries — such as the Asia and Europe packages — remain the best value for multi-country itineraries, with 5G access included in all of them.
